    Responsible-use notes

    No personal identifiers or protected attributes are features. Every block has a human-readable reason. Borderline scores go to manual review, not auto-decline. Labels arrive late in reality, so the retraining loop needs monitoring for label bias. Results are on synthetic data and overstate real-world separability.
